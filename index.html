What this can't catch

Static rule checking detects roughly a third of WCAG success criteria failures. The keyboard and reflow checks push past that, but they narrow the gap — they don't close it.

Nothing here can judge whether alt text is accurate, whether focus order matches the visual reading order, whether error messages are clear, or how a page behaves with a screen reader actually running. Content behind a login, inside a PDF, or reachable only after interaction isn't assessed at all.

A clean automated result is necessary but not sufficient for conformance — and the report says so, on its own pages. That's deliberate: it's legally safer, and it's the natural opening for the manual audit you sell on top.
